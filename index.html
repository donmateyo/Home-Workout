⚠️ Stop / Progress Rules
✅Mild discomfort or tightness during exercise is okay.
🛑Sharp or radiating pain → stop that exercise immediately.
🦵If symptoms travel farther down the leg, stop that lift and note it.
🔄If next-day symptoms are worse, repeat the last good week instead of progressing.
🐌All reps slow and controlled: 2-3 sec down, pause, 2 sec up.
🚫Avoid: swings, cleans, snatches, floor deadlifts, heavy RDLs, twisting, sit-ups.
🧍Keep a neutral spine at all times. Brace before every rep.
Workout A Exercises
1Goblet Box Squat — KB at chest, sit to box, neutral spine
2Dumbbell Floor Press — Lying on floor, elbows touch ground each rep
3Supported 1-Arm Row — Hand on bench, pull to hip, no rotation
4Glute Bridge — Shoulders on floor, squeeze glutes at top, hold 1 sec
5Farmer Carry — Tall posture, brace core, even steps
Workout B Exercises
1Split Squat — Stationary lunge position, vertical torso
2Dumbbell Floor Press — Same as Workout A
3Supported 1-Arm Row — Same as Workout A
4Side Plank — From knees if needed, build time gradually
5Farmer Carry — Same as Workout A
